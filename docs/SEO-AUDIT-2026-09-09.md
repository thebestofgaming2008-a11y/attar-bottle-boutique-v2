# BADR SEO audit — 9 September 2026

## Scope

Public storefront, product pages, guides, brand/contact/policy pages, search feeds, and indexing protections. No payment keys, payment implementation, database schema, customer records, order records, promotions, or admin settings changed. Existing design retained; two small groups of relevant guide links added.

## Implemented

- Brand-first homepage title connects BADR and House of BADR with attar perfume oils in India. Shop metadata targets shopping/comparison intent separately.
- Removed hardcoded starting-price claims from home/shop/default social descriptions so price edits do not make these misleading.
- Product fallback descriptions use the product's visible scent notes. Admin-written SEO titles/descriptions and social images remain authoritative.
- Published homepage layout and film configuration are included in server-rendered HTML, not deferred exclusively to client JavaScript.
- Product pages no longer revive deleted seed products. Related recommendations use the active catalog. Empty catalogs stay empty; page-loader outages surface as errors instead of fabricated stock/products.
- Products with optional slugs can resolve through their active catalog ID. An ID URL redirects permanently to the product's slug when one exists. Product canonical, offer and listing URLs are encoded consistently.
- Sitemap uses only the current active catalog, including products without slugs, and absolute image URLs. An unavailable catalog returns a retryable, non-cacheable 503 rather than stale invented product URLs.
- Merchant feed uses stable catalog IDs instead of mutable SKUs, does not misrepresent SKUs as manufacturer part numbers, and describes packs as sets rather than a single default 6 ml bottle. Feed changes can cause Merchant Center to treat previous SKU-keyed items as new items; inspect Diagnostics after its next fetch.
- Error responses now also carry noindex/no-store headers. Existing protections for admin/account/checkout/order/tracking remain intact.
- Product and shop pages link to relevant existing guides. Article visible update dates come from their actual article metadata, not a fixed display string.
- Owner checklist corrected: do not repeat completed DNS verification or add a duplicate analytics beacon.
